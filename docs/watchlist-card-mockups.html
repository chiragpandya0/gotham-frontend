Watchlist entry card: options

Same data in every option: plate or name, list type, priority, optional description, inactive state. First card is selected, second is inactive, fourth has a description.

A. Priority bar, two lines

Priority is a coloured bar on the left edge (like the alert queue), so no priority chip is needed. Type sits on the right of line one, tags below.
